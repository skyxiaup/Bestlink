Frequently Asked Questions

How do I initiate a warranty claim?

Contact our technical support team at info@bestlinktec.com with your order number and product serial number. We will guide you through the RMA process.

What is the warranty period for your products?

Most industrial switches come with a 3-year warranty, while wireless products and accessories have a 2-year warranty. Extended warranties are available upon request.

Do you offer technical support for product configuration?

Yes, our technical support team can assist with product configuration, network setup, and troubleshooting via email, phone, or online meeting.

Can I get custom firmware for your products?

Yes, we offer custom firmware development for OEM/ODM customers. Contact our sales team to discuss your requirements.

How long does it take to process a warranty claim?

Typically, warranty claims are processed within 5-7 business days. Complex cases may require additional time for evaluation.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 100 countries worldwide. We provide professional customs documentation support for international orders.

Need Technical Support?

Our support team is ready to assist you. Contact us for any technical questions or warranty inquiries.
